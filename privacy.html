Chainglow privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Chainglow ("the game") is made by Salvatore Minaudo ("we", "us"), Melbourne, Australia. This policy explains, in plain English, what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • No account needed: we never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your progress stays on your device: levels, stars, coins and settings.
  • The free version shows ads. Our ad partner uses information about your device to choose and measure ads. On iPhone, it only uses your device's advertising identifier if you allow tracking when the app asks.
  • Anonymous statistics: we collect gameplay statistics and error reports to make the game better.
  • We never sell information that identifies you.

What the game stores on your device

  • Progress:
  • your levels, stars and coins;
  • the blast skins you own;
  • your Daily Board results and streak.
  • Settings, including whether reminders are on and the hour you usually play (so a reminder can arrive at a good time).

This stays on your device and is deleted when you delete the game. Settings → Reset progress clears progress at any time. Purchases are kept: they belong to your App Store account.

Information collected when you play

Gameplay statistics and error reports (analytics)

We use GameAnalytics to understand how the game is played, for example which levels are too hard, how many people come back the next day, and whether a change made the game better.

It collects:

  • Game events:
  • levels started, passed or failed, and scores;
  • Daily Board plays;
  • use of boosters and hints;
  • ads watched and purchases made;
  • offers shown, and taps on the share button.
  • Error reports: if the game hits a technical error, a short description of it, so we can fix it. These contain no personal information.
  • Device information: device model, operating system version, language, the app version, and your approximate country. The country is worked out from your internet address, which isn't stored for this purpose.
  • An identifier for your installation, made by GameAnalytics. On iPhone this can include the "identifier for vendor". It is shared only between apps from the same developer, and can't be used to track you across other companies' apps.

This information is not linked to your name or contact details, because we don't have them. See GameAnalytics' privacy policy.

Ads

The free version shows:

  • Short videos you choose to watch for a reward, for example an Extra Spark, double coins, a hint, or saving your streak.
  • Occasional ads between levels.

Ads are provided by AppLovin MAX, which shows AppLovin's own ads. If we add other ad networks through MAX later, we'll list them here.

To show and measure ads, these companies may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when the app asks;
  • device information: model, operating system, language, screen size, battery and network type;
  • your internet address (IP), which they use to work out your approximate location;
  • how you interact with ads: what was shown, and whether you watched or tapped it.

Your choice on iPhone:

  • After your first few tries, the app asks whether it may track you (Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt).
  • If you choose Ask App Not to Track, ads are still shown, but they aren't personalised using your advertising identifier.
  • You can change this later in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Where the law requires consent (for example in the EU, the UK and some US states), the app first asks for your consent to personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices inside the game.

See AppLovin's privacy policy.

Purchases

Remove Ads and the Starter Pack are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment, and we never see your card details or Apple ID. We learn only that the purchase happened, so we can give you what you bought and restore it if you reinstall.

Reminders (notifications)

If you allow notifications, the game reminds you when a new Daily Board is ready, at most once a day.

  • These reminders are scheduled by the game on your phone. No server is involved and nothing is sent anywhere.
  • You can turn them off in Settings → Reminders inside the game, or in iPhone Settings → Notifications → Chainglow.

Game Center (iPhone)

If you're signed in to Apple Game Center:

  • the game sends your Daily Board best scores and achievements to it, so you can compare scores with other players;
  • Game Center shows your Game Center nickname to other players on the leaderboard.

This is run by Apple, under Apple's privacy policy. You can sign out of Game Center in iPhone Settings.

Sharing your Daily result and rating the game

  • Sharing: the SHARE button opens your phone's own share sheet with a short text, like "Chainglow #42 💥 1050 pts", and a link to the game. You choose where it goes. The game doesn't post anything itself or read your contacts.
  • Rating: now and then, the game may ask iOS to show Apple's rating prompt. Apple handles it, and we don't see whether you rated the game or what you wrote.

Children

Chainglow is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has used the game and information about them was collected, contact us and we'll help delete it.

How long information is kept

  • On your device: until you delete the game or reset your progress.
  • GameAnalytics: keeps gameplay statistics under its own retention policy, then deletes or anonymises them.
  • Ad partners: keep data under their own policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the Australian Privacy Act, the EU and UK GDPR, or Californian law), you may have the right to:

  • ask what information is held about you;
  • have it corrected or deleted;
  • object to its use.

To act on these rights:

  • Contact us. We don't hold your name or contact details, so to find "your" analytics data we need your installation's identifier, and we'll explain how to find it.
  • Uninstall the game to stop all collection.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. For important changes, we'll also mention it in the game's release notes.
