Support

Chainglow is a one-tap chain reaction puzzle: tap once, set off a blast, and watch the chain pop through the board.

Need help? Email sammymin683@gmail.com and we'll get back to you, usually within two working days. Please mention your iPhone model and what happened.

Common questions

I bought Remove Ads or the Starter Pack on another phone

Open Settings → Restore Purchases in the game, signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I stop the reminders?

In the game, Settings → Reminders → OFF, or in iPhone Settings → Notifications → Chainglow.

Can I change my ad tracking choice?

Yes: iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Where the law requires it, the game also has Settings → Privacy choices.

How do I start again?

Settings → Reset progress (tap twice). Your purchases are kept.

A level feels too hard

Keep going: after a few tries the game lowers the target a little, and after two fails you can watch a short video for a hint that shows where and when to tap.
